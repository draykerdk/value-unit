> The boundary where external value can become accountable internal capacity without becoming a price for reputation.

**Dktron represents available resources and their distribution. It also provides a bridge for exchange with the external economy.**

The Value Unit project researches Dktron as a stable representation of available resources: how resources enter Drayker, how they are distributed among funds and projects, how projects decide what to spend within their authorization, and how value can be exchanged externally through bridge infrastructure built by Drayker. Reputation remains a different system.

## The problem it addresses

Drayker needs to represent what resources exist, where they are distributed and how external value becomes accountable common capacity without importing an internal market into membership.

**How it works today.** Resources arrive through ordinary external arrangements — grants, donations, contracts, purchases. Nothing internal represents them, and nothing accounts for their distribution.

**What would change.** Dktron represents available resources and their distribution to project and category funds. A project decides how to spend the capacity it receives within its purpose, permissions and accountability constraints. Proposed Drayker bridges connect Dktron to external negotiation without making wealth a price for reputation or membership.

**Why the rest depends on it.** Without a stable representation of resources and funds, common capacity cannot be accounted, allocated or audited — and external support cannot become internal capability.

## Where this stands

The repository publishes the current architectural formulation of Dktron and its relation to the economy of capacity. Value, reputation and DAF federative points are distinct concepts. No Dktron is issued, priced, sold or exchangeable; no stability mechanism or bridge has been demonstrated. There is no operating monetary policy, liquidity, income programme or automatic resource allocation.

Stable value is an architectural requirement — it is intended to make resource accounting and external exchange coherent, and its mechanism still requires specification and evidence. It is not a present guarantee.

The first validation is an accounting one, not a monetary one: a transparent record of contributions, needs, decisions and accountability, before any unit that moves. Dk optimizes none of it until the human rules have been written down by people.

Nothing described here is implemented. This repository exists so that the first
document about it has somewhere to live and someone can argue with it in public.

## Scope

- Dktron as the proposed stable representation of resources
- Project and category funds with project spending authority
- External negotiation through proposed Drayker bridge infrastructure
- Value, reputation and DAF points as distinct concepts
- An accountable resource ledger before any unit that moves
- Stable value as an architectural requirement requiring specification and evidence

## Not in scope

- A token, currency, investment, security or financial product available today
- A current guarantee of stability, appreciation, yield, liquidity, exchangeability, income or support payments
- A price for reputation, membership, voting rights or human worth
- An internal market where basic capacity is sold to members
- Automated allocation by Dk without constitutional rules, accountable authorization and review
- Unverified rates, stabilization mechanisms or monetary claims treated as current guarantees

## How it fits the whole

How resources become accountable internal capacity — the accounting and constraint layer for common capacity, not a score of persons.

Dktron represents resources available to Drayker and distributed among project and category funds. Projects decide spending within the authority they receive; proposed Drayker bridges connect external negotiation. Contribution creates public evidence that may inform trust and deliberation; it does not purchase membership, human worth or automatic access. Reputation is contextual, multidimensional memory, and DAF federative points are a provisional rule inside the transitional federation — neither converts into the other. Every project and application may carry a fund. [Dk](https://dk.drayker.org) can organize evidence, model scenarios and recommend how categories and causes relate, but it neither owns nor allocates common resources: accountable member governance decides, inside systemic safeguards.

## First functions

These are concrete and unclaimed. Any of them can be opened as an issue and delivered
by one person.

1. Specify the resource and fund ledger: who takes part, what the unit of record is,
   who reviews it, and what ends it. This is deliberately an accounting exercise, and
   it comes before everything else here.
2. Define project spending authority and review inside an allocated fund.
3. Model one external bridge end to end.
4. Specify and test the stable-value requirement.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/draykerdk/.github/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)
